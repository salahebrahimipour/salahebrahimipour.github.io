A brief introduction

I am a Computational Archaeologist! Currently, I work as a Researcher and Database Manager at the University of Oxford. https://www.arch.ox.ac.uk/people/salah-ebrahimipour

As an archaeologist specialising in Iranian archaeology, I have cultivated a robust academic foundation through my B.A. and M.A. studies. During my PhD studies I integrated field evidence with innovative computational methods, such as computational methods in defining best possible route using circuit theory, least-cost path analysis, as well as remote sensing and GIS spatial analysis.

My research focuses on the archaeological landscape of western and northwestern Iran, as well as northern Mesopotamia, spanning from the Early Bronze Age to the end of the first millennium BCE. I am particularly interested in exploring socio-ecological dynamics and human interactions within this region.

My methodological approach combines traditional archaeological techniques with advanced digital tools. I employ statistical analysis, computational modeling such as Machine Learning and GIS applications to gain deeper insights into ancient societies and their environments. This interdisciplinary approach allows me to contribute to the development of new archaeological methods while addressing fundamental questions about past human behaviors and cultural exchanges.
